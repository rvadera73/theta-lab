## How to Read This Section

### Line 1: Framework Overview
```
Framework: $100,000/month net = $1.2M/year target (at 60% close costs)
```
**Meaning:** Your annual goal is $1.2M net premium. At 60% transaction costs, you keep 40% of gross premium.

### Line 2: Regime Adjustment
```
Regime: BEAR (applies 70% of base)
```
**Meaning:** Market conditions are cautious. Targets scale to 70% of the base (easier to hit).

### Line 3: Adjusted Targets
```
Adjusted Target: $175,000 gross / $70,000 net
```
**Meaning:** In BEAR regime, you need $175K gross premium/month to keep $70K net (after 60% costs).

### Lines 4-6: YTD Performance
```
YTD Performance: $387,000 net (5 months)
Monthly average: $77,400
Annualized pace: $928,800 ⚠️ BELOW TARGET
```

**Meaning:** 
- You've collected $387K net YTD
- That's $77.4K/month average
- If you maintain this pace, you'll hit $928.8K/year (vs $1.2M target)
- Status shows you're below the $1.2M target

## What This Section DOES Tell You

- ✅ Framework: $1.2M annual target confirmed
- ✅ Regime adjustment: How to scale targets based on market conditions
- ✅ Gross vs net split: 60% costs, 40% kept
- ✅ Monthly targets by regime: $175K (BEAR) / $212.5K (SIDEWAYS) / $250K (BULL)
- ✅ Account allocation: Each account's proportional target
- ✅ YTD pace: On track or below target
- ✅ Annualized projection: If you maintain current pace, where you'll end the year

## How to Use It

### Daily Check
Compare your **YTD pace** to **$1.2M annual target**:
- If pace > $1.2M → ✅ Exceeding goal
- If pace $1.1M–$1.2M → ✅ On track
- If pace < $1.1M → ⚠️ Below target (need acceleration)

### Weekly Check
Check which **regime** applies and what that month's **adjusted target** is.
- BEAR: $70K/month needed
- SIDEWAYS: $85K/month needed
- BULL: $100K/month needed

### Monthly Check
At month-end, see if each **account hit its target**.
- Account A: Did it produce $106.75K gross (BEAR) or proportional amount?
- Others: Did they hit their combined $68.25K gross (BEAR)?

### When Regime Shifts
Watch this section update automatically:
- If VIX spikes → regime shifts to BEAR → targets drop to 70%
- If market recovers → regime shifts to BULL → targets rise to 100%
- You'll see the new adjusted targets immediately
