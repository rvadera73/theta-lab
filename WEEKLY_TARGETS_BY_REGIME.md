## How to Use These Targets

### Daily Tracking
At end of each trading day, compare:
- **Actual gross premium collected today** vs **Daily pace target**
- If behind: acceleration needed mid-week
- If ahead: maintain quality, don't overleverage

### Weekly Checkpoint (Monday morning)
- Review last week's gross and net premium
- Calculate weekly close cost ratio (gross - net) ÷ gross
- Compare to 60% target
- Adjust closing strategy if ratio is too high

### Monthly Review (1st of month)
- Compare actual net premium to regime-adjusted monthly target
- Calculate YTD variance
- Adjust strategy if trending below target
- Check if regime shift needed

## Quick Decision Rules

**If BEAT daily pace by 15%+:**
- ✅ Maintain strategy (you're executing well)
- ⚠️ Don't over-leverage (respect margin limits)
- 🎯 Quality over quantity (higher conviction > more contracts)

**If MISS daily pace by 15%+:**
- 🔴 Acceleration needed
- Check: IVR gate (need ≥40 for new entries?)
- Check: Existing position capacity (can you add size to held positions?)
- Check: Position holding period (are you closing too early? = lower gross)

**If close cost ratio is 70%+ (too high):**
- You're paying too much in spreads/slippage
- Reduce trade frequency
- Use better entry prices (don't chase)
- Let profitable positions run longer (don't close at 20% profit)

**If close cost ratio is <50% (too low):**
- You're closing very profitably (good news)
- But may be leaving money on table
- Consider holding winners slightly longer
- Or increase position count (you're not at maximum leverage)
