### **Why MSFT/AMZN Beat NVDA:**

**1. THE POWER CONSTRAINT IS MSFT/AMZN'S COMPETITIVE MOAT, NOT CONSTRAINT**

WRONG intuition: "Power is a constraint, bad for AI growth"
CORRECT intuition: "Power is a constraint, creates scarcity in cloud services, allows MSFT/AMZN to raise cloud prices"

How it works:
- Big Five AI capex ($660-690B total) is NOT limited by chip availability (TSMC has capacity)
- It IS limited by power availability (data center power takes 2-3 years to build)
- MSFT and AMZN own data center real estate and power contracts
- As power becomes scarce, they can increase Azure/AWS pricing (higher margins)
- NVDA gets squeezed because customers (MSFT/AMZN) negotiate harder on chip prices to offset cloud cost increases

**VERDICT: Power constraint is BULLISH for MSFT/AMZN, BEARISH for NVDA.**

**2. CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION RISK IN NVDA IS REAL**

NVDA customer base (FY 2025):
- Meta: 25-30% of revenue
- MSFT: 15-20% of revenue
- Google: 10-15% of revenue
- Amazon: 5-10% of revenue
- Others: 20-25%

If any single customer (Meta) delays capex → NVDA guidance cuts 20-30%
If two customers (Meta + MSFT) shift to AMD for cost savings → NVDA guidance cuts 40%+

MSFT/AMZN have NO comparable concentration risk:
- No single customer is >5% of cloud revenue
- Enterprise SaaS customer stickiness is higher than chip loyalty
- Switching costs (migration, retraining, data gravity) lock in customers

**VERDICT: NVDA concentration risk is material. MSFT/AMZN have lower execution risk.**

NVDA current state (assumed Jun 15):

MSFT current state (assumed):

- P/E multiple: 25-30x (reasonable for 25%+ EPS growth)
- Analyst upside: +10-15% (to $460-490)
- Downside if macro disappoints: -10-15% (to $360-385)
- Risk/reward: 1:1 (balanced)

**Conclusion:** MSFT upside is 10-15% with 25-30x multiple expansion. NVDA upside is 15-20% but requires maintaining 40-50x multiple (risky, could compress).

**4. POWER CONSTRAINT CREATES SUSTAINABLE COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE FOR MSFT/AMZN**

This is the KEY insight:

NVDA story: "More chips for AI capex → NVDA margins expand"
PROBLEM: This assumes unlimited data center power, which is FALSE

MSFT/AMZN story: "Scarce power → we control power supply → we raise cloud prices → higher margins + AI capex continues at higher cost"
BENEFIT: This is self-reinforcing. As power becomes MORE scarce, MSFT/AMZN capture MORE value.

**VERDICT: MSFT/AMZN have a STRUCTURAL moat in AI capex access. NVDA has a CYCLICAL boom (peaks when growth slows).**

5. **Regulatory risk** — China export controls could shift NVIDIA capex mix (consumer AI training to edge devices, lower margin)

### **The MSFT/AMZN Thesis is SAFER because:**

✓ Diversified customer base (no single customer >5%)
✓ Pricing power increasing (power scarcity = cloud price increases)
✓ Execution track record (proven capex deployment)
✓ Valuation safety (25-30x multiple leaves room for upside)

✓ Margin expansion path (power constraint = margin driver for cloud ops)

**DECISION RULE:**
