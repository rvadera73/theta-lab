QUESTION 2: MSFT/AMZN BETTER THAN NVDA FOR AI TREND? WHY?

VERDICT: ✅ YES, MSFT/AMZN are better risk-adjusted. Here's the data-driven comparison:

THE CORE INSIGHT: Power Constraint Creates Pricing Power for Cloud Operators
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**NVDA's Problem (Concentration Risk):**
- Customer concentration: Meta 25-30%, MSFT 15-20%, GOOGL 10-15%, AMZN 5-10% = 55-75% from Big Five
- Risk: If Meta or MSFT delays capex → NVDA guidance cuts 20-30%+
- Supply constraint: TSMC capacity is fixed; NVIDIA can't increase supply faster than demand
- Result: NVIDIA becomes a commodity supplier with limited pricing power

**MSFT/AMZN's Advantage (Pricing Power):**
- Power availability is THE binding constraint for AI capex, not chips
- MSFT owns Azure data center real estate + power contracts → Can increase cloud pricing
- AMZN owns AWS infrastructure + renewable power contracts → Can increase AWS pricing
- Result: As power becomes scarce, they raise cloud prices → Higher margins on same capex spending

**HOW THE POWER CONSTRAINT WORKS:**

Enterprise AI capex cycle:
1. Company budgets $100M for AI (fixed budget)
2. Power to run AI systems: Limited (scarce)
3. They need chips (NVDA): Available, but power is bottleneck
4. They rent cloud from MSFT/AMZN: Pay premium for "guaranteed power"
5. MSFT/AMZN raise cloud prices by 10-15% → Same $100M budget buys less compute
6. NVIDIA sees same unit demand but LOWER total $$$ per customer (customer is budgeted, so they optimize price/performance)

**Result:** Power scarcity = margin EXPANSION for cloud ops (MSFT/AMZN), margin COMPRESSION for chips (NVDA).
