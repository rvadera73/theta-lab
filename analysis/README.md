## The Core Framework: Conviction Strangle

### Why This Works

1. **Conviction names have strong fundamentals** → Your puts don't expire worthless
   - 85%+ historical win rate proves this

2. **Deep ITM puts guarantee assignment** → You OWN the equity (feature, not risk)
   - Assignment is planned (not a surprise)

3. **Assigned equity immediately generates call premium** → You lock in 2-3 week profits
   - Assignment happens, you sell call next day
   - Call loses 50% value in 2-3 weeks (take profit)
   - Capital freed, cycle repeats

4. **2-3 week exit timing beats buy-hold** → Superior capital efficiency
   - Don't hold shares for weeks; don't hold calls to expiration
   - In/out in 14-21 days = cycle 4x per quarter

### The Math

**Per Strangle Cycle (1 put + 1 call):**
- Put premium on entry: $1,200-1,300
- Put assignment: 100% (expected, planned)
- Call premium on entry: $1,500-2,500 (sold immediately after assignment)
- Call profit target: 50% max value = $750-1,250
- **Total profit per 2-week cycle: $2,200-3,300**
- **Quarterly (4 cycles): $8,800-13,200**
- **Annualized (12 cycles): $26,400-39,600** per account
- **With 3-5 active accounts: $79,200-198,000**

**To reach $1.2M:** Need 30-45 cycles per year (6-9 per account) across 4-5 accounts. Achievable if you deploy 5 accounts with same gem model.

## Key Success Factors

### 1. Conviction Depth (Not Breadth)
- Focus on 3-5 "gem" names with proven fundamentals
- Don't spread thin across 20+ low-quality names
- Deep ITM puts on quality names = high win rate

### 2. Assignment Acceptance (Mindset Shift)
- Assignment is NOT failure; it's inventory building
- You want puts to assign (planned)
- Call selling on assigned shares is where the real premium lives

### 3. Quick Capital Rotation (Speed is Feature)
- Sell call within 24 hours of assignment (don't wait)
- Exit call at 50% max profit in 2-3 weeks (don't be greedy)
- Re-deploy capital immediately (don't sit in cash)

### 4. Margin Discipline (Buffer for Volatility)
- Maintain margin <60% at all times
- CRWD/AXON can move 10-15% in a day
- Need buffer to hold through assignment without forced liquidation

### 5. Thesis Monitoring (Know When to Stop)
- Check each gem weekly for news/earnings/guidance changes
- Thesis breaker = close immediately (don't average down)
- This is your only "risk control" (not stop-losses on options)

## FAQ: Common Questions

**Q: What if AXON doesn't assign?**  
A: Delta >0.99 means it will. If for some reason it doesn't by June 30, close at 50% profit and redeploy. Don't hold deep ITM puts hoping for more decay.

**Q: What if call gets assigned early (before 2 weeks)?**  
A: Congrats, you got paid fast. Record the profit, move on. Early assignment on calls is rare (no dividend on CRWD/AXON/COIN typically) but take it as a win.

**Q: Can I do this with margin instead of multiple accounts?**  
A: Yes, but riskier. $1.2M annual from 1 account @ $500K deployed = 2.4x leverage. Doable but margin calls on 10% dip = forced liquidation. Safer to use 4-5 accounts @ 1.5x each.

**Q: What if the market crashes?**  
A: ITM puts lose value (good—they become deeper ITM). Your assignment is locked in. On assigned calls, you get called away = realize profit. Downside is OK in this model.

**Q: What about taxes?**  
A: Short-term capital gains on all profits (held <1 year). That's OK; it's all in trading accounts (not retirement accounts for the high-volume names). Plan for 37% fed + state on profits.
